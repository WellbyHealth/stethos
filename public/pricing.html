FAQ

Pricing questions

Something else? Email our team.

What is a credit?

The unit every call is priced in. Searches cost a fraction of a credit per result, a full provider record costs one, and optional fields add to it. Every response includes a header showing the credits that call used.

When are credits used?

Only when data comes back. A search with no matches, or an enrich on an NPI we don't have, costs nothing.

Do unused credits roll over?

On Build, unused monthly credits roll over for one month. Top-up credits last 12 months.

What happens if I run out?

Calls return a clear error, never a surprise bill. You can top up any time, or turn on auto top-up with a monthly cap.

Is there a free plan?

Every account starts with 1,000 free credits, no card needed, so you can try every endpoint on your own use case.

Do you offer flat files?

Yes, on Enterprise. We deliver the full dataset, or the slice you need, to your warehouse on a schedule.

Do I need a BAA?

No. Stethos only holds data about healthcare professionals and organizations, not patients.
