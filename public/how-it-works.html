Provenance

      
Every field shows where it came from.

      
An agent that acts on healthcare data needs to know how far to trust it. Stethos is built to keep the source and the last-verified date on every single field, not just on the record.

      
    
        
  • Government sources first
    NPPES, CMS utilization, post-acute and Care Compare files, Open Payments and state licensing boards are the backbone.
    • 
        
  • Licensed and web sources on top
    Payer directories, price transparency files, practice websites and health system job posts fill the gaps.
    • 
        
  • Conflicts kept, not hidden
    When two sources disagree, you see both and which one we picked, so your agent can decide.
    • 
        
  • NPI as the key
    Join cleanly to the claims, CRM and EHR data you already have.
    • 
      

    

    
  





  

    

      
Signals

      
The changes worth acting on.

      
Most databases tell you what's true today. Stethos is built to record every change as an event, so you can ask what changed this week, or get told as it happens.

    

    

      
Change feedSample data

      
    
    
    
    
      
      
    • Moves
      affiliation.changed
      A clinician joins or leaves a group, hospital or health system.
      • 
      
    • Licenses
      license.issued · license.lapsed
      A new state license, or one that expired or was restricted.
      • 
      
    • Networks
      network.joined · network.left
      A practice shows up in, or drops out of, a payer's directory.
      • 
      
    • Locations
      location.opened
      A new clinic, practice site or service line comes online.
      • 
      
    • Leaders
      org.exec_hire
      A health system names a new CMO, CNO, CFO or service line head.
      • 
      
    • Hiring
      org.hiring_spike
      An organization posts far more clinical roles than usual.
