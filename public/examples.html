What sales and marketing agents do with Stethos.

Ten sales and marketing workflows your team could run from the AI assistant and CRM it already uses. Each one shows the question, what the agent does, the data behind it and where the result lands. Names and numbers are examples.

01 · Medtech sales

Who should I call this week?

When Every Monday, or whenever a rep asks.

Ask“Find independent spine surgeons in my territory doing 50+ fusions a year, and tell me who changed anything recently.”

What the agent does

  1. Searches surgeons by specialty, territory and Medicare procedure volume
  2. Ranks them by recent changes: a new group, a new surgery center, new hires
  3. Creates or updates the contacts in Salesforce with a one-line talking point

Stethos data it uses

  • Medicare procedure volumes
  • Affiliations
  • Change events
  • Practice contacts

Where the result lands

Salesforce or HubSpot, plus a weekly list in Claude

02 · Health IT sales

Reach the new buyer in their first 90 days

When A target health system names a new CIO, CMIO or CFO.

Ask“Tell me when any health system in my territory hires a new CIO or CMIO, and draft a first note for each.”

What the agent does

  1. Watches every account in the territory for executive changes
  2. Adds the system's EHR, size and recent news to the alert
  3. Creates a task in the CRM with a short, specific first note for the rep to edit

Stethos data it uses

  • Organizations
  • Executive changes
  • Certified health IT data
  • Change events

Where the result lands

A task in Salesforce or HubSpot, and a Slack message to the rep

03 · Health IT marketing

Find every hospital on a given EHR

When You launch an integration for a specific EHR.

Ask“List every hospital and large group running Oracle Health, with their IT leaders, and load them into HubSpot.”

What the agent does

  1. Finds organizations by the certified EHR they report using
  2. Adds the organization's leadership and locations
  3. Creates a HubSpot list for the campaign

Stethos data it uses

  • ONC certified health IT data
  • EHR vendors' published customer lists
  • Organizations
  • Contacts

Where the result lands

HubSpot or Salesforce

04 · Marketing

Build an ABM account list with the buying team

When Planning a quarter's account-based campaign.

Ask“Give me the 200 health systems in the Southeast with 5+ hospitals and a cardiology service line, with the cardiology leaders at each.”

What the agent does

  1. Searches organizations by type, size, region and service lines
  2. Adds the clinicians and leaders in cardiology at each account
  3. Scores accounts by fit and loads them into your marketing tool as a list

Stethos data it uses

  • Organizations
  • Service lines
  • Affiliations
  • Leaders

Where the result lands

HubSpot, Salesforce or your ad platform's audience upload

05 · Pharma

Map the key opinion leaders for a launch

When A new launch or an upcoming congress.

Ask“Who are the most active clinicians in this drug class in the Northeast: publishing, running trials and prescribing?”

What the agent does

  1. Finds clinicians by prescribing volume in the class
  2. Adds their publications, trials and grants
  3. Flags industry relationships so medical affairs can review them first

Stethos data it uses

  • Part D prescribers
  • Publications and trials
  • NIH grants
  • Open Payments

Where the result lands

Your CRM or a medical affairs spreadsheet

06 · Sales operations

Draw territories on real accounts

When Annual planning, or a new rep starts.

Ask“Split Texas into four territories with a similar number of high-volume orthopedic surgeons in each.”

What the agent does

  1. Counts target surgeons and facilities by county and metro
  2. Weights them by procedure volume and current affiliation
  3. Proposes balanced territories and lists the top accounts in each

Stethos data it uses

  • Medicare procedure volumes
  • Practice locations
  • Affiliations

Where the result lands

A territory sheet for sales ops, and account owners in the CRM

07 · Field marketing

Fill the room for a dinner or workshop

When A regional event or a booth at a congress.

Ask“Find gastroenterologists within 30 miles of our Chicago dinner who perform colonoscopies and haven't met with us.”

What the agent does

  1. Finds clinicians by specialty, procedure and distance from the venue
  2. Removes anyone already in the CRM as a customer or recent contact
  3. Builds the invite list with practice contacts and sources

Stethos data it uses

  • Specialty and procedure data
  • Practice locations
  • Practice contacts
  • Your CRM

Where the result lands

A campaign list in your marketing tool

08 · Revenue operations

Keep the CRM true without data entry

When Continuously, on every account and contact.

Ask“Check every physician contact in Salesforce and fix anyone who moved, retired or changed groups.”

What the agent does

  1. Matches each contact to an NPI-keyed record
  2. Updates employer, location and specialty, with the source for each change
  3. Watches the list and sends a webhook whenever a contact changes again

Stethos data it uses

  • NPI Registry
  • Affiliations
  • Change events
  • Licenses

Where the result lands

Salesforce, HubSpot or your data warehouse

09 · Staffing sales

Find the systems that need staff now

When Weekly, for the business development team.

Ask“Which hospitals in Arizona are posting the most ICU nursing roles, and who runs nursing there?”

What the agent does

  1. Ranks nearby health systems by recent clinical job posts
  2. Adds the chief nursing officer and other leaders at each
  3. Creates the accounts and next steps in your CRM

Stethos data it uses

  • Health system job posts
  • Organizations
  • Leaders
  • Change events

Where the result lands

Your CRM, plus a weekly list in Claude

10 · Health system marketing

Grow referrals where your network is thin

When A service line's referrals are down.

Ask“Which independent primary care doctors near our hospitals aren't affiliated with us, and who recently joined a competing group?”

What the agent does

  1. Finds primary care clinicians near each hospital
  2. Separates those affiliated with you, with competitors, or independent
  3. Flags recent moves so physician liaisons can reach out first

Stethos data it uses

  • Affiliations
  • ACO participation
  • Change events

Where the result lands

Your physician relations CRM
