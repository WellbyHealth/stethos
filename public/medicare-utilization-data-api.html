FAQ

Common questions

Related: Docs: data sources · Physician data API · Medtech sales

Where does this data come from?

CMS's Medicare Provider Utilization and Payment data. They summarize 100% of Original Medicare fee-for-service claims at the provider level, and CMS suppresses small counts to protect patients.

How current is it?

CMS releases these files once a year, covering a past year, so they describe volume and mix rather than this week's activity. Stethos shows the data year on every number and pairs it with fresher signals like affiliation and license changes.

Does it include Medicare Advantage or commercial claims?

No. These files cover Original Medicare only. Claims-level data, including Medicare Advantage, requires CMS research access, which Stethos plans to pursue.

Is there patient data in it?

No. Every number is aggregated to the provider, and CMS removes counts under 11. Stethos returns provider data only.
