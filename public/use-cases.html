Prospecting and targeting

Find the right accounts and people, and decide who to call first.

Medtech · Sales

Who should I call this week?

When Every Monday, or whenever a rep asks.

“Find independent spine surgeons in my territory doing 50+ fusions a year, and tell me who changed anything recently.”

  • Medicare procedure volumes
  • Affiliations
  • Change events
  • Practice contacts
How it works
  1. Searches surgeons by specialty, territory and Medicare procedure volume
  2. Ranks them by recent changes: a new group, a new surgery center, new hires
  3. Creates or updates the contacts in Salesforce with a one-line talking point

Where the result lands: Salesforce or HubSpot, plus a weekly list in Claude
