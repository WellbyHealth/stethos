Data coverage

Eleven kinds of provider data, one record per NPI.

Stethos is built on government files first: the NPI Registry, CMS Medicare files and state licensing boards. Payer, web and hiring signals are layered on top, and Stethos agents call practices and check websites to confirm what the files can't. Every field names its source and the date it was last checked.

Data categories

  • Identity

    Public

    One NPI per clinician or organization, with taxonomy and practice address.

    Sources NPPES NPI Registry

    Refresh Weekly

  • Licenses

    Public

    License status, expiry, discipline and nurse compact privileges.

    Sources State licensing boards, 50 states + DC

    Refresh Varies by board

  • Affiliations and organizations

    Public

    Who works where, group rosters, ownership and parent systems.

    Sources Care Compare, Provider of Services file, ACO participation, organization NPIs

    Refresh Monthly to annual

  • Clinician volume

    Public

    Procedures, Medicare patients, prescribing and equipment orders per clinician.

    Sources Medicare Physician & Other Practitioners, Part D Prescribers, DMEPOS

    Refresh Annual

  • Facility volume

    Public

    Discharges per hospital; volume and patient mix for home health, hospice, SNF, IRF and LTCH.

    Sources Medicare Inpatient & Outpatient Hospitals, Post-Acute Care & Hospice

    Refresh Annual

  • Quality and financials

    Public

    Ratings, quality scores, beds, staffing, revenue and costs.

    Sources Care Compare star ratings and measures, hospital and SNF cost reports

    Refresh Monthly to quarterly

  • Payers and networks

    Public

    Which plans each provider is in, negotiated rates and MA share by market.

    Sources Payer directories, price transparency files, Medicare Advantage enrollment

    Refresh Monthly

  • Industry relationships

    Public

    Payments from drug and device makers to clinicians and teaching hospitals.

    Sources Open Payments

    Refresh Annual

  • Live signals

    Public

    New locations, moves, leadership changes and hiring spikes.

    Sources Practice websites, health system job posts

    Refresh Continuous

  • Agent-verified

    Stethos agents

    Calls practices and checks websites to confirm address, phone, accepting patients and network participation when sources disagree or go stale.

    Sources Stethos voice and web agents

    Refresh On change, and on a schedule

  • Claims-level Medicare

    Planned

    Current referral flows, shared patients, discharge destinations and market share.

    Sources 100% Medicare Part A, B and MA claims, via CMS VRDC

    Refresh Quarterly

Claims-level Medicare data needs CMS research access, which Stethos plans to apply for. Everything else comes from public files today. The full source list is in the docs.
