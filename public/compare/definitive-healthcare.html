Definitive is a platform for analysts. Stethos is an API for agents.

Definitive Healthcare is one of the largest healthcare commercial intelligence platforms, with deep data on hospitals, physicians and claims, sold mostly to enterprise teams. Stethos is built for developers and the agents they ship.

With Definitive Healthcare, you typically

  • Buy an annual platform subscription through sales
  • Search and filter in the web platform
  • Pay extra to pipe data into your CRM or warehouse
  • Work from periodic refreshes and exports
  • Get deep claims and market analytics

With Stethos, you're built to

  • Add it to Claude in five minutes, no sales call
  • Pay per tool call and per field, not per seat
  • Get change events as they happen
  • See the source and verified date on every field
  • Use REST and webhooks for the rest of your stack
