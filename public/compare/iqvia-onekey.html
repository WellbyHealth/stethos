OneKey is reference data for life sciences. Stethos is built for agents.

IQVIA OneKey is a long-established reference dataset of healthcare professionals and organizations, delivered through APIs, portals and data services and connected to IQVIA's other datasets. Stethos is built for developers who want to start small and pay per call.

With IQVIA OneKey, you typically

  • Sign a multi-year agreement through sales
  • Use OneKey as the master data behind your CRM
  • Connect it to other IQVIA datasets
  • Request data corrections through validation workflows
  • Budget for an enterprise contract

With Stethos, you're built to

  • Add it to Claude in five minutes, no sales call
  • Pay per tool call and per field, not per seat
  • Get change events as they happen
  • See the source and verified date on every field
  • Use REST and webhooks for the rest of your stack
