The problem

Healthcare data goes stale the day you buy it.

  • Provider databases refresh on a schedule. Clinicians change groups, locations and networks all year.

  • The best data is public, but it's scattered across NPPES, CMS files, state boards and payer directories, each with its own format.

  • Licenses are issued and lapse state by state, on dozens of separate board websites.

  • Payer directories disagree with each other, and with where the doctor actually practices.

  • Your agent writes to a surgeon who left that group last spring, and nobody finds out until the email bounces.

The platform

One clean record for every provider, and every change to it.

  • Search

    Built to search every clinician and organization in the US by specialty, place, license, affiliation or network.

    Wide, cheap queries so your agent can look at everyone before it picks anyone.

  • Enrich

    Built to turn an NPI into a full record with licenses, affiliations, networks and contacts.

    Additive pricing: the base record first, then only the extra fields you ask for.

  • Organizations

    Built to map health systems, groups and clinics and who works at each one.

    Locations, rostered clinicians, leadership, open roles and parent–child ownership.

  • Watch

    Built to tell your agent when a record changes, so it moves first.

    Affiliation changes, new licenses, network moves, executive hires and hiring spikes.

  • MCP first

    Built to plug straight into your agents as an MCP server, in five minutes.

    REST and webhooks are there too, for the parts of your stack that aren't agents.

FAQ

      
Common questions

      
      
Still curious? Ask our team or read the docs.

    

    

      
What is Stethos?
An MCP server, and API, for US healthcare provider data. It's built to keep one clean, current record for every clinician, practice, hospital and health system, stitched together from public registries and licensed sources, and to tell your software the moment one of those records changes.

      
Who is it for?
Teams building products on top of healthcare provider data: AI sales agents for medtech and pharma, provider directory and network teams at payers and digital front doors, clinical staffing and recruiting platforms, healthcare investors, and digital health apps that need to know who practices where.

      
Where does the data come from?
Public sources like the NPPES NPI Registry, CMS provider and facility files, Open Payments and state licensing boards, plus payer directories, price transparency files, practice websites and health system job posts. Every field carries its source and the date it was last checked.

      
Does Stethos handle patient data?
No. Stethos indexes healthcare professionals and organizations, not patients. There is no PHI in the API, so you don't need a business associate agreement to use it.

      
How fresh is it?
Sources are re-checked on a rolling schedule, and every change is recorded as an event you can query or subscribe to with a webhook. Each record shows when it was last verified, so your agent can decide how much to trust it.

      
How is this different from Definitive Healthcare or IQVIA OneKey?
Both are established, deep datasets, sold mostly through annual contracts to large commercial teams. Stethos is built API-first for developers and agents: pay per call, start free, and get change events instead of quarterly refreshes. See Stethos vs Definitive and Stethos vs IQVIA OneKey.

      
How do I connect it?
Copy the Stethos MCP server URL and add it to your client: as a custom connector in Claude, or in the MCP config of Cursor or your own agent. Sign in, and the tools appear. It takes about five minutes and there's no sales call.

      
Is there a REST API too?
Yes. The same data is available over REST, with webhooks for change events, for the parts of your stack that aren't agents.

      
How does pricing work?
Per tool call, in credits. A search costs a fraction of a credit per result, enriching a provider costs more, and you only pay for the fields you ask for. Every account starts with 1,000 free credits, Build is a monthly plan, and Enterprise adds bulk files and volume pricing.

      
Can we get the whole dataset as a file?
Yes, on Enterprise. We deliver flat files to your warehouse on a schedule, alongside API access for real-time lookups.

      
Is it live?
Stethos is in development. The tools on this site are a preview of what we're building, and the results shown are sample data. Join the early access list and we'll send you access as soon as it's ready.
