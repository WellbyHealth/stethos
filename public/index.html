FAQ

Common questions

Still curious? Ask our team or read the docs.

What is Stethos?

Current, sourced healthcare data for AI agents, delivered as an MCP server and API. Stethos resolves government, payer and web sources into one record for every US clinician, practice, health system and payer network, maps the referral patterns, ownership and Medicare volume around them, puts a source and a checked date on every field, keeps records current with verification agents, and tells your agent when something changes.

Can't my agent just ask a general AI assistant?

General AI assistants can find some healthcare data, but they answer from whatever web page or registry copy they come across, often months or years old, with no source and no date. Provider data changes constantly: clinicians move groups, licenses lapse, networks change. Stethos re-checks its sources, shows where every field came from and when it was last checked, and tells your agent when a record changes.

Does it work with our CRM and AI tools?

Yes, that's the point. Stethos runs as an MCP server inside Claude, ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot Studio, Cursor or your own agent, and its REST API and webhooks keep records current in Salesforce, HubSpot, Attio or any CRM or data warehouse. There's no separate dashboard to adopt.

Where does the data come from?

Government files first: the NPPES NPI Registry, CMS's Medicare utilization files for clinicians, prescribers, hospitals, home health, hospice and nursing facilities, Care Compare ratings and affiliations, ACO participation, cost reports and Open Payments, plus state licensing boards. Payer directories, price transparency files, practice websites and health system job posts are layered on top. Every field carries its source and the date it was last checked. See the full list in the docs.

Does Stethos handle patient data?

No. Stethos indexes healthcare professionals and organizations, not patients. There is no PHI in the API, so you don't need a business associate agreement to use it.

How fresh is it?

Sources are re-checked on a rolling schedule, and every change is recorded as an event you can query or subscribe to with a webhook. Each record shows when it was last verified, so your agent can decide how much to trust it.

How is this different from Definitive Healthcare or IQVIA OneKey?

Both are established, deep datasets, sold mostly through annual contracts to large commercial teams. Stethos is built for teams that work through AI assistants and CRMs: pay per call, start free, and get change events instead of quarterly refreshes. See Stethos vs Definitive and Stethos vs IQVIA OneKey.
