FAQ

Common questions

Still curious? Ask our team or read the docs.

What is Stethos?

Current, sourced healthcare data for AI agents, delivered as an MCP server and API. Stethos resolves government, payer and web sources into one record for every US clinician, practice, health system and payer network, puts a source and a checked date on every field, keeps records current with verification agents, and tells your agent when something changes.

How does it help sales, marketing and investing?

Your reps and agents can build target lists by specialty, setting, EHR or territory, enrich accounts and contacts with sourced fields, and get an alert the week a target changes jobs, a practice joins a system or a health system names a new leader. Those changes become your outreach triggers, and the records in your CRM stay current without anyone re-checking lists. Investment teams use the same tools to map a market, size a platform and track portfolio companies.

Can't my agent just ask a general AI assistant?

General AI assistants can find some provider data, but they answer from whatever web page or registry copy they come across, often months or years old, with no source and no date. Provider data changes constantly: clinicians move groups, licenses lapse, networks change. Stethos re-checks its sources, shows where every field came from and when it was last checked, and tells your agent when a record changes.

Does it work with our CRM and AI tools?

Yes, that's the point. Stethos runs as an MCP server inside Claude, ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot Studio, Cursor or your own agent, and its REST API and webhooks keep records current in Salesforce, HubSpot, Attio or any CRM or data warehouse. There's no separate dashboard to adopt.

Who is it for?

Anyone who sells into, markets to or invests in healthcare: medtech and pharma commercial teams, health IT, software and services sellers, healthcare marketers, investors and deal teams, startups finding their first customers, home care, hospice and post-acute agencies keeping their referral networks warm, and clinical staffing firms. Stethos gives their people, agents and CRM a current view of who practices where, who buys, and what just changed. Provider directory teams use the same data.

Where does the data come from?

Government files first: the NPPES NPI Registry, CMS's Medicare utilization files for clinicians, prescribers, hospitals, home health, hospice and nursing facilities, Care Compare ratings and affiliations, ACO participation, cost reports and Open Payments, plus state licensing boards. Payer directories, price transparency files, practice websites and health system job posts are layered on top. Every field carries its source and the date it was last checked. See the full list in the docs.

Does Stethos handle patient data?

No. Stethos indexes healthcare professionals and organizations, not patients. There is no PHI in the API, so you don't need a business associate agreement to use it.

How fresh is it?

Sources are re-checked on a rolling schedule, and every change is recorded as an event you can query or subscribe to with a webhook. Each record shows when it was last verified, so your agent can decide how much to trust it.

How is this different from Definitive Healthcare or IQVIA OneKey?

Both are established, deep datasets, sold mostly through annual contracts to large commercial teams. Stethos is built for teams that work through AI assistants and CRMs: pay per call, start free, and get change events instead of quarterly refreshes. See Stethos vs Definitive and Stethos vs IQVIA OneKey.

How do I connect it?

Copy the Stethos MCP server URL and add it to your client: as a custom connector in Claude, or in the MCP config of Cursor or your own agent. Sign in, and the tools appear. It takes about five minutes and there's no sales call.

Is there a REST API too?

Yes. The same data is available over REST, with webhooks for change events, for the parts of your stack that aren't agents.

How does pricing work?

Per tool call, in credits. A search costs a fraction of a credit per result, enriching a provider costs more, and you only pay for the fields you ask for. Every account starts with 1,000 free credits, Build is a monthly plan, and Enterprise adds bulk files and volume pricing.

Can we get the whole dataset as a file?

Yes, on Enterprise. We deliver flat files to your warehouse on a schedule, alongside API access for real-time lookups.
