Find referral partners for post-acute care and agencies.

Home health, hospice and skilled nursing grow through referrals, and referral sources change constantly: a hospitalist group turns over, a physician joins a new practice, a new clinic opens across town. Stethos is built to give your liaisons and their agents a current map of who refers in your market, from CMS post-acute and utilization files, Care Compare and practice records, and to tell them when a source moves or a new one opens.

Post-acute care and agencies

Stethos is built to

Find the hospitals, physicians and practices that send patients to care like yours, and hear when a referral source moves, opens or changes hands.

Data you'd use

  • Hospitals and skilled nursing facilities by market, with discharge volume
  • Physicians who refer to home health and hospice, from Medicare data
  • Care Compare ratings for you and your competitors
  • Practice and liaison contact details

Signals you'd watch

  • A referring physician joins a new group
  • A hospital or practice opens nearby
  • A competing agency's rating changes

Example call

What it looks like in code.

One request against the Stethos API. Responses carry the source and last-verified date on every field. See the quickstart for authentication, watchers and the MCP server, and pricing for what each call costs.

curl https://api.stethos.health/v1/providers/search \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $STETHOS_KEY" \
  -d '{
  "specialty": [
    "internal medicine",
    "family medicine",
    "cardiology"
  ],
  "county": "Maricopa, AZ",
  "fields": [
    "affiliations",
    "practice_contact",
    "post_acute_referrals"
  ]
}'

FAQ

Post-acute care and agencies: common questions

Something else? Email our team.

Where does referral data come from?

From CMS public files, such as Medicare home health, hospice and skilled nursing data, and physician utilization files that show which clinicians order or certify post-acute care. Each value shows its source and date, and no patient-level data is included.

Can liaisons see competitors?

Yes. Care Compare ratings, service areas and Medicare volume are public for home health agencies, hospices and nursing facilities, so you can see who else serves a market and how they score.

Does it work with our referral CRM?

Your agent can write referral sources and next steps into Salesforce, HubSpot or a post-acute CRM, and watchers send a webhook when a source changes so the list stays current.
