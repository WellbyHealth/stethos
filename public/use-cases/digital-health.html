Provider search for digital health and AI apps.

Find-a-doctor, referral and care navigation features all need the same thing underneath: a current, searchable record of who practices where and which networks they're in. Stethos is built to be that layer, through a REST API for your app and an MCP server for your agents.

Digital health and AI apps

Stethos is built to

Power provider search, referrals and scheduling in your app with one API, and let your agents look up any clinician through MCP.

Data you'd use

  • Provider search by specialty, location and network
  • Organization and location records
  • NPI-keyed records that join to your data

Signals you'd watch

  • A provider in your app changes location
  • A practice joins a network your users have
  • A record your app shows is re-verified

Example call

What it looks like in code.

One request against the Stethos API. Responses carry the source and last-verified date on every field. See the quickstart for authentication, watchers and the MCP server, and pricing for what each call costs.

curl https://api.stethos.health/v1/providers/search \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $STETHOS_KEY" \
  -d '{
  "specialty": "dermatology",
  "city": "Austin",
  "state": "TX",
  "network": "[Payer A]",
  "limit": 10
}'

FAQ

Digital health and AI apps: common questions

Something else? Email our team.

Can my AI agent use Stethos directly?

Yes. The MCP server exposes search, enrich, organization and recent-changes tools to Claude and other MCP clients.

Is it HIPAA-covered data?

No. Stethos holds provider and organization data only, with no patient information, so you can use it without a business associate agreement.

How do I keep my app's listings fresh?

Create a watcher for the providers your app shows. When one changes location, network or license, you get a webhook and can update the listing.
