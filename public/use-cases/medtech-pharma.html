Reach the right surgeon at the right moment: when they join a new group, start at a new facility or take over a service line. Stethos is built to keep a current record for every target and tell your agents the week it changes.

Who should I call this week?

When Every Monday, or whenever a rep asks.

Ask“Find independent spine surgeons in my territory doing 50+ fusions a year, and tell me who changed anything recently.”

Example call

What it looks like in code.

One request against the Stethos API. Responses carry the source and last-verified date on every field. See the quickstart for authentication, watchers and the MCP server, and pricing for what each call costs.

curl https://api.stethos.health/v1/providers/search \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $STETHOS_KEY" \
  -d '{
  "specialty": "orthopedic surgery",
  "state": "AZ",
  "affiliation_type": "independent_group",
  "fields": [
    "affiliations",
    "practice_contact",
    "industry_payments"
  ]
}'

FAQ

Medtech and pharma sales: common questions

Something else? Email our team.

Can I filter physicians by procedure focus?

Search filters include specialty and sub-specialty, practice setting, affiliation and license state. Procedure-focus signals come from public sources such as CMS data and practice websites, and each one shows its source.

Does it include Open Payments data?

Yes. Enrich can add an Open Payments summary for a physician as an optional field, so your team can see existing industry relationships before reaching out.

Can it update our CRM automatically?

Watchers send a webhook when a target's affiliation, location or license changes, so your CRM or agent can update the record and queue outreach without anyone re-checking lists.
