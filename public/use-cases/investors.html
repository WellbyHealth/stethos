Home / Use cases / Healthcare investors

Healthcare market signals for investors, as they happen.

In healthcare services, the leading indicators are clinicians and locations: who is joining a platform, where new sites are opening and which groups are losing people. Stethos is built to turn those changes into events you can track across a portfolio or a target list.

Healthcare investors

Stethos is built to

Track practice roll-ups, clinician headcount at portfolio and target companies, and new entrants in a market as it happens, not a quarter later.

Data you'd use

  • Organizations with ownership and parent–child links
  • Rostered clinicians per organization over time
  • Locations by market

Signals you'd watch

  • A group adds or loses clinicians
  • A new location opens in a target market
  • An executive hire at a target

Workflows

What teams do with it.

Investors · Diligence

Market diligence in an afternoon

When A deal comes in for a regional orthopedic or GI group.

Ask“Map every orthopedic group in metro Phoenix: size, ownership, hospital ties, volume, quality and payer mix.”

What the agent does

  1. Maps every group, its clinicians and its parent organization
  2. Adds Medicare volume, quality scores and Medicare Advantage share by county
  3. Writes a short memo with a spreadsheet of the underlying records and sources

Stethos data it uses

  • Organizations
  • Affiliations
  • Medicare utilization
  • Cost reports
  • MA enrollment

Where the result lands

A memo in Claude and a spreadsheet for the deal team

Investors · Portfolio monitoring

Know when a portfolio or target company changes

When Continuously, across the portfolio and the pipeline.

Ask“Tell me whenever one of our portfolio companies or the 40 groups on our target list adds or loses clinicians, opens a site or hires an executive.”

What the agent does

  1. Watches each organization's clinicians, locations and leaders
  2. Rolls changes into a weekly digest, with the source for each
  3. Flags big moves, like a group losing several surgeons, the day they show up

Stethos data it uses

  • Organizations
  • Affiliations
  • Change events
  • Locations

Where the result lands

A weekly digest in Slack or email, plus your deal CRM

Example call

What it looks like in code.

One request against the Stethos API. Responses carry the source and last-verified date on every field. See the quickstart for authentication, watchers and the MCP server, and pricing for what each call costs.

curl https://api.stethos.health/v1/orgs/enrich \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $STETHOS_KEY" \
  -d '{
  "name": "Example Ortho Partners",
  "state": "AZ",
  "fields": [
    "children",
    "rostered_clinicians",
    "locations"
  ]
}'

FAQ

Healthcare investors: common questions

Something else? Email our team.

Can I track clinician headcount over time?

Organization records include rostered clinicians, and change history shows who joined and left and when, so you can chart headcount for a group or platform.

Does it show ownership?

Parent and child organizations are linked where public sources show the relationship, with the source on each link.

Can I get the data as a file?

Yes. Enterprise includes flat files delivered to your warehouse on a schedule, alongside the API.
