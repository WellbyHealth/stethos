Directory listings drift out of date because clinicians move, groups merge and networks change faster than anyone reports it. Stethos is built to check each listing against current sources, show where they disagree, and alert your team when a listed clinician changes location, license or network.

For developers

What it looks like in code.

One request against the Stethos API. Responses carry the source and last-verified date on every field. See the quickstart for authentication, watchers and the MCP server, and pricing for what each call costs.

curl https://api.stethos.health/v1/providers/enrich \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $STETHOS_KEY" \
  -d '{
  "npi": "1234567890",
  "fields": [
    "practice_contact",
    "licenses",
    "networks"
  ]
}'

FAQ

Common questions

Something else? Email our team.

How does Stethos help with directory accuracy?

Each listing can be compared with current sources: practice websites, state boards, CMS data and other payer directories. Every field shows its source and last-verified date, and conflicts are returned rather than hidden.

Can it replace calling practices?

It's built to cut down how many you need to call. Change events and source conflicts point your team at the listings most likely to be wrong, instead of re-verifying everyone on a fixed schedule.

Does it include patient data?

No. Stethos only holds information about clinicians and organizations in their professional capacity.
