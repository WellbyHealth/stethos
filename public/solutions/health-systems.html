For developers

What it looks like in code.

One request against the Stethos API. Responses carry the source and last-verified date on every field. See the quickstart for authentication, watchers and the MCP server, and pricing for what each call costs.

curl https://api.stethos.health/v1/providers/search \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $STETHOS_KEY" \
  -d '{
  "specialty": "cardiology",
  "city": "Nashville",
  "state": "TN",
  "fields": [
    "affiliations",
    "aco",
    "medicare_volume"
  ],
  "limit": 25
}'

FAQ

Common questions

Something else? Email our team.

Can I see which physicians refer to us?

Not from claims yet. Claims-level referral flows need CMS's research data environment, which Stethos plans to apply for. Today Stethos uses group, hospital and ACO ties, which explain much of who refers to whom, plus Medicare volume.

Does it work with our physician relations CRM?

Yes. Enrich and watch through the API or MCP server, and send changes to your CRM with webhooks.

Is there patient data?

No. Stethos holds data about clinicians and organizations only.
