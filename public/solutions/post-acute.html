For developers

What it looks like in code.

One request against the Stethos API. Responses carry the source and last-verified date on every field. See the quickstart for authentication, watchers and the MCP server, and pricing for what each call costs.

curl https://api.stethos.health/v1/providers/search \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $STETHOS_KEY" \
  -d '{
  "match": "referrers.csv",
  "county": [
    "Dallas, TX",
    "Collin, TX"
  ],
  "fields": [
    "affiliations",
    "practice_contact",
    "org_size",
    "post_acute_referrals"
  ]
}'

FAQ

Common questions

Something else? Email our team.

We don't have a CRM. Does it still work?

Yes. Upload a spreadsheet of your referrers, or export the list from your EHR or agency software, and Stethos matches each one to a current record. Results come back as a list, a weekly email or a spreadsheet; a CRM is optional.

We're private pay. Is the referral data still useful?

The Medicare data shows which physicians order home health and hospice, which is a strong sign they refer patients who need care at home, whoever pays. The rest, such as practices, hospitals, affiliations and contact details, applies to any agency.

Can it help with big medical centers?

Yes. Large academic and hospital campuses are broken down by department, practice and location, with who works where, so a marketer can see which parts of a campus they already know and which they haven't reached.

Where does referral data come from?

From CMS data, such as Medicare home health, hospice and skilled nursing data, and physician utilization files, plus practice and organization records. Each value shows its source and date. No patient-level data is included.
