For developers

What it looks like in code.

One request against the Stethos API. Responses carry the source and last-verified date on every field. See the quickstart for authentication, watchers and the MCP server, and pricing for what each call costs.

curl https://api.stethos.health/v1/providers/search \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $STETHOS_KEY" \
  -d '{
  "org_type": "health_system",
  "state": "OH",
  "fields": [
    "locations",
    "health_it",
    "executives",
    "affiliates"
  ]
}'

FAQ

Common questions

Something else? Email our team.

Can I filter health systems by EHR?

Where it's published, yes. Certified health IT use comes from ONC and CMS sources, and each value shows its source and date. Where no source exists, the field is left empty rather than guessed.

Does it include executive contacts?

Stethos returns named executives and service line leaders from sources such as organization websites and press releases, with the source for each. It doesn't sell personal emails or phone numbers.

Will it work in Salesforce or HubSpot?

Yes. Your agent calls Stethos tools and writes the results into your CRM, and watchers can send a webhook when an account changes so the record and next step update on their own.
