For developers

What it looks like in code.

One request against the Stethos API. Responses carry the source and last-verified date on every field. See the quickstart for authentication, watchers and the MCP server, and pricing for what each call costs.

curl https://api.stethos.health/v1/providers/search \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $STETHOS_KEY" \
  -d '{
  "specialty": "registered nurse",
  "license_state": "TX",
  "fields": [
    "licenses",
    "affiliations"
  ]
}'

FAQ

Common questions

Something else? Email our team.

Which licenses are covered?

State licenses for physicians, nurses and other clinicians, with status and source, gathered from state licensing boards. Coverage will be published by state and profession at launch.

Can I see which organizations are hiring?

Organization records include open clinical roles, and the org.hiring_spike event fires when an organization's postings rise well above its normal level.

Can I find clinicians who just got a new license?

Yes. The license.issued event fires when a new state license appears for a clinician you watch, or you can query recent license events by state.
