Physician data API

A physician data API that keeps up with physicians.

Physicians change groups, add locations, pick up licenses and join networks all year. Stethos is built to keep one current record per physician, keyed on the NPI, and to tell your product or sales agent when that record changes.

FAQ

Common questions

Related: Medtech sales · NPI lookup API · Pricing

How many physicians does it cover?

Stethos is built to cover every US physician with an NPI, from the NPPES registry, with other sources layered on top. Coverage of each field will be published at launch.

Can I get contact details?

Practice contact details, such as phone, fax and practice address, are an optional field with their sources. Stethos does not sell personal home addresses or personal phone numbers.

Can I sync it to a CRM?

Yes. Enrich records on demand and subscribe to watchers so your CRM is updated when a physician moves, instead of re-importing lists.
