FAQ

Common questions

Related: Docs: connect in five minutes · NPI lookup API · Pricing

Why not just ask a general AI assistant?

General assistants answer from whatever page they find, often out of date, with no source or date. Stethos returns current records with the source and last-checked date on every field, and alerts your agent when something changes.

How is this different from free NPI MCP servers?

Free servers wrap the NPI registry. Stethos starts from the same NPI and adds state licenses, current affiliations, payer networks and organization data, with a source on every field and alerts when a record changes.

Do I need to talk to sales?

No. It's self-serve: 1,000 free credits, no card, and plans and top-ups you can manage yourself.

Does it handle patient data?

No. Stethos only holds data about clinicians and organizations, so there's no PHI and no business associate agreement needed.

What does it cost?

Each tool call costs credits: a search result is 0.05, a provider record is 1 plus optional fields. Build is $149 a month for 20,000 credits.
