Privacy notice

Last updated November 5, 2026 · Draft for review

What this covers

This notice covers the Stethos marketing website. Stethos is in development. Before the API opens, we will publish terms and a data policy that cover the product itself.

What we collect on this site

If you email us or join early access, we receive your name, work email, company and anything you choose to tell us. Our hosting provider records standard technical information, such as browser type and referring page, in normal server logs. This site does not use advertising or analytics cookies.

How we use it

Only to contact you about Stethos, including early access, research conversations and product updates. We do not sell your information or share it for others' marketing.

About the data in the API

The Stethos API is built to hold information about healthcare professionals and organizations in their professional capacity, drawn from government registries, licensed sources and organization websites. It does not hold patient information. Clinicians who want to correct or ask about their record can email us.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete your information, or to stop contacting you, at any time by emailing team@stethos.health.

Contact

Questions about this notice can go to team@stethos.health.
