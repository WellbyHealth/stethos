FAQ

Health system data API: common questions

Related: Healthcare investors · Docs: organizations · Pricing

Where does organization data come from?

CMS provider and facility data, organization NPIs, health system websites and job posts, and press releases for leadership changes. Every field shows its source.

Can I see who works at a hospital?

Organization records include rostered clinicians drawn from affiliation data, and the list updates as clinicians join or leave.

Can I track leadership changes?

Yes. The org.exec_hire event fires when an organization names a new executive or service line leader.
